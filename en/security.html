Information security and data deletion

Last updated: August 2026

RemindOCR uses HTTPS, secured storage, and role-based access control.

Account and data deletion

  1. Sign in at app.remindocr.com/en/login.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Choose Close account and delete data and confirm as instructed.

Deletion is irreversible and includes documents and data originating from third parties (such as Gmail).
