Frequently asked questions

Have a question about your documents? Email our team.

Who is RemindOCR for?

Inspection providers, equipment service businesses, and office teams that manage client documents and recurring follow-up. It brings reports, equipment records, key dates, and email reminders into one workflow.

What can I upload?

Upload PDF, Word, Excel, and image files, including inspection reports, certificates, contracts, and invoices. Extraction results depend on the document’s layout and quality. Use the trial to check your own documents.

Should I review the dates AI extracts?

Yes. Check extracted dates and client details against the original before relying on them for follow-up. For a contract, confirm whether you need to act before the expiration date, such as a cancellation or notice deadline.

How do reminders work?

RemindOCR schedules email reminders ahead of tracked dates, with intervals such as 90, 60, and 30 days. Review your reminder settings and recipients, and customize the email template for your clients.

Do I need to connect Google?

You can start by uploading documents directly. Gmail import, Google Calendar sync, and Google Drive sync are optional connections. Availability depends on your plan.

How can I evaluate it before paying?

Start a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. Try your own reports, review the extracted fields, and set up reminders. See plans and limits before choosing a subscription.
