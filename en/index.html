Frequently asked questions

Have a question about your documents? Email our team.

Who is RemindOCR for?

Anyone who has to keep up with renewals, bills and paperwork: business owners, office managers, freelancers and service teams. Inspectors and equipment businesses get dedicated inspection and equipment tools.

How do documents get in?

Upload PDF, Word, Excel or image files, photograph a paper document on your phone, import attachments and receipts from Gmail, or forward an email to your personal address (Pro plan).

Do I need to check what AI extracts?

Yes. Check dates, amounts and names against the original before relying on them. Imports that arrive automatically and look uncertain wait in a review queue until you approve or remove them.

How do reminders work?

RemindOCR sends email reminders ahead of tracked dates, for example 90, 60 and 30 days before. You choose recipients and customize the template. Browser notifications are optional.

Can it track invoices and receipts?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Invoices and receipts are imported from Gmail, receipts are matched to the invoices they paid, and expenses are shown in a graph and a filterable table.

Which languages are supported?

The app works in Hebrew, English and Russian, and reads Hebrew, English and Russian documents. Switch language at any time from the user menu.

How can I evaluate it before paying?

Start a 30-day free trial with no credit card. Try your own documents, review the extracted fields and set up reminders. See plans and limits before choosing a subscription.
